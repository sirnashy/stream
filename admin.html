Quick reference manuals for setting up the most popular high-CPM networks on your live streaming site.

Monetag Settings

  • **Smart Tag**: Paste your main Monetag Smart Tag code directly into the **Global Head Injection** area. Enable the switch.
  • **Native Banners**: Generate banner widgets (728x90 / 300x250) in your Monetag panel, then paste the script markup in **Ad Placements**.
  • **Vignette / On-Click Ads**: Best placed in the **Global Body Injection** tab.

Adsterra Settings

  • **Display Banners**: Get the code for 728x90 and 320x50 sizes. Paste them inside the **Top / Bottom Player** slots.
  • **Dynamic Iframe Sandbox**: Adsterra codes containing `document.write` are safely captured inside our custom dynamic sandbox iframe, preventing page crashes.
  • **Popunder Ads**: Place your Adsterra Popunder script (e.g. `invoke.js` source) in the **Global Body Injection** to trigger popups site-wide.

Adcash Settings

  • **Autotag Integration**: Grab your site Autotag code and paste it in **Global Head Injection**. It automatically manages push, overlays, and interstitial ads.
  • **Display Ads**: Paste specific Adcash banner zone scripts in the matching slots.
